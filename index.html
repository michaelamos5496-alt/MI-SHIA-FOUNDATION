About the foundation

As much as we can, as often as we can.

Mi Shia focuses on reaching out to rural areas where basic necessities are hard to come by, including food, clothes, stationery and toiletries. As a small but growing organization, we strive to do as much as we can, as often as we can.

Our mission

To help Ghana, our home, in any way possible.

We are dedicated to making a tangible difference in the lives of those in underserved communities by providing essential resources and support. We aim to uplift and empower individuals and families, ensuring they have the basic necessities to live with dignity and hope.

Our vision

Free medical aid for rural villages.

We aim to extend our impact by providing free medical aid to these villages. We aspire to establish simple healthcare services and small clinics, bringing essential medical care closer to those who need it most. With YOUR HELP, we hope to achieve this in the near future, creating healthier and more resilient communities.

Our objectives

  • Regularly distributing food, clothing, stationery and toiletries to rural areas.
  • Raising awareness and funds to support our initiatives.
  • Collaborating with local and international partners to expand our reach and resources.
  • Establishing free medical aid services and small clinics in underserved villages to improve access to healthcare.

The founder

Dr. Djifa van Staalduinen

Dr. Djifa van Staalduinen is a dynamic and passionate young woman ready to take the world by storm and bring others along for the ride. After earning her medical degree in 2021, she chose to channel her energy into a different kind of healing. Driven by a lifelong desire to uplift those in need, she founded the Mi Shia Foundation.

Growing up, Djifa was deeply affected by the hardships faced by those with fewer resources. Frustrated by the inefficiencies of other organizations, she decided to create a foundation where every effort directly benefits those in need. While she views this work as a heartfelt hobby, Djifa’s enthusiasm and dedication are infectious.

Through the Mi Shia Foundation, she hopes to inspire others to tag along and join her mission to make the world a better place, one compassionate act at a time.

News 12 March 2025

Orange Sanitary Pads collaborates with Mi Shia Foundation to promote Menstrual Hygiene

Mi Shia Foundation has partnered with Orange Tulip to celebrate the World Menstrual Hygiene Day on Monday May 27, 2024 at the Osu Presbyterian Secondary School.

Mi Shia Foundation has partnered with Orange Tulip to celebrate the World Menstrual Hygiene Day on Monday May 27, 2024 at the Osu Presbyterian Secondary School.

The event which took place at the Osu Presbyterian Church Chapel, gathered over 500 female students with the aim to educate young women on menstrual hygiene under the theme “menstrual friendly world.”

According to the team, this collaborative effort aimed to empower young women with knowledge and resources to manage their menstrual health effectively.

Speaking at the event, founder of Mishia Foundation, Miss Dzifa renowned for her presence on TikTok and a qualified medical doctor, hinted at the significance of maintaining personal hygiene, particularly during menstruation.

Mr. Michael Eshun, Chief Operations Officer at Orange Sanitary, emphasized that periods are natural and not a cause for shame or disgust.

The students actively participated in a Q&A session, seeking clarification and sharing insights. Some students received gifts, while all were provided with packs of sanitary pads courtesy of Mishia Foundation and Orange Charities, the corporate social responsibility arm of Orange Sanitary Pads.
